# EUR-27 and World Cup distribution

**EUR-27 accounts for 16,343,635 retained downloader memberships (28.97% of the world total)
and 111,970 uploader memberships (20.75%).** France leads downloader counts;
the Netherlands leads uploader counts. These are IP–torrent memberships across
493 canonical torrents, sampled June 29–November 3, 2026, not unique residents,
completed downloads or viewers.

The fixed AAM boundary contains twenty-seven countries. It excludes the United
Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Russia, Türkiye and separately coded
territories. All torrents contribute to the primary view, regardless of audio
language or the teams playing.

## Weeks, catalog coverage and the post-final interval

The final falls at the end of week 3. Week 19 contains only November 2–3;
all earlier weeks contain seven dates. The catalog grows during sampling.
The lines below show within-week memberships, which must not be added to
cumulative membership totals or interpreted as a live television audience.

**90.5% of downloader membership-days occur after the final.**
That compares 107 post-final dates with 21 dates through the final. It is not
the percentage of people who downloaded after the event.

A fixed-catalog check gives useful context. Hold the **412 torrents** with
retained geography in week 3 constant, then compare two complete seven-date
weeks: July 13–19 and October 26–November 1. EUR-27 downloader memberships
fall from **873,283 to 748,003 (-14.3%)**.
Other torrents contribute 145,629 memberships in week 18.
This uses per-torrent retention, separately from the weekly aggregate lines.
It supports continued circulation in the sample, without establishing growing
live demand or completed downloads.
