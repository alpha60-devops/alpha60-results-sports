### EUR-27 evidence, downloads and limits

The AAM fixed EUR-27 boundary is shared with the regional page. The reviewed
public evidence is bounded and selective; no paid panel or private contract was
obtained. Source-access failures are distinct from absence of a measurement.
FIFA's roster supplies license chains, not prices, language terms or an audited
full-match allocation for every broadcaster. Source records retain unknown
provider, universe, timestamps, out-of-home and consolidation fields.

The original nineteen-fixture cache product is immutable. Two separately
hashed new fixtures use the same inventory, classification and IPinfo databases.
All four original comparison groups recompute unchanged. Twenty-one fixtures
are available for all twenty-seven country codes at one, seven and twenty-eight
observed calendar days, under both IP unions and membership sums. Missing
country observations within this cache are observed zeros; missing broadcast
measurements remain null or an explicit status. Exclusions are separate,
overlapping hosting/VPN flags. No peer addresses are published.

Spearman ranks average ties and suppress estimates below three fixtures.
Leave-one-out ranges are descriptive sensitivity, not confidence intervals.
Published highlights select successful fixtures and cannot represent all
matches. No TV audience is added to an IP count. Original currencies remain
unconverted; no FX-based cross-market purchase ranking is claimed.
